About

          

        


        

          

            
Security works best when it helps a business move forward. That belief shapes most of what I do.

            
I started out in hands-on IT support, so I know how systems and people actually behave day to day. I bring that into every risk assessment, policy and awareness programme I work on. A big part of my role is sitting down with senior leaders, understanding what they’re trying to achieve, and turning security requirements into clear decisions they can act on with confidence.

            
What excites me now is GRC engineering: the idea that compliance should be built and automated, not managed through spreadsheets and screenshots. I’m working towards using automation and practical engineering to help organisations achieve and maintain Cyber Essentials, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001, so that security and responsible AI governance become measurable and part of everyday operations.

            
I’m curious by nature, so I keep my technical skills sharp with hands-on labs and investigation projects like the ones on this page. I also enjoy sharing what I learn, whether that’s running a session for peers or helping someone take the next step in their career.
